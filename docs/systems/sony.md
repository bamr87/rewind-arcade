# Sony

> The PlayStation family: optical discs, then a handheld that is also a disc (UMD), then a cart-based Vita.

## PlayStation (`psx`)

1994 disc. Aliases: `ps1`, `playstation`. Default core: SwanStation; PCSX-ReARMed is offered on Linux / Pi. US BIOS `scph5501.bin` is required for a complete setup and is **never** downloaded.

Catalog treats PlayStation as a shelf and price-guide platform. Disc identification is not in the dump parsers.

## PSP (`psp`)

2004 UMD / digital. Emulate manifest present and enabled.

## Later PlayStation (`ps2`, `ps3`, `ps4`, `psvita`)

Catalog-only ids so inventory CSVs and price guides resolve. PS2/PS3 emulation is standalone (PCSX2, RPCS3) and is not wired through libretro in this tree. Vita is a cartridge-shaped handheld on the shelf list.
