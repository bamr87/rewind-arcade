# Atari

> VCS through Jaguar, plus the 8-bit computers and the Lynx.

## Atari 2600 (`atari2600`)

1977 cartridge. Stella. Enabled in a fresh frontend.

## Atari 8-bit (`atari800`)

1979 floppy / tape / cart. 400, 800, XL, XE. Core: `atari800_libretro`. Optional OS ROMs. Enable when you keep `.atr` / `.xex` images.

## Atari 5200 (`atari5200`)

1982 cartridge. Electrically close to the 8-bit computers; `a5200` is the dedicated core. Alias: `5200`.

## Atari 7800 (`atari7800`)

1986 cartridge. Enabled.

## Lynx (`lynx`)

1989 handheld cartridge. Handy. Optional `lynxboot.img` from a Lynx you own. Alias: `atarilynx`.

## Jaguar (`jaguar`)

1993 cartridge. Virtual Jaguar; compatibility is uneven. Prefer `.j64`. Alias: `atarijaguar`.
