# Arcade and everything else

## Arcade (`arcade`)

PCB romsets. Aliases: `mame`, `fbneo`. Default: FinalBurn Neo; MAME 2003-Plus as alternative. Romsets are version-specific — the two cores do not share a set. Catalog tracks arcade as a platform on a shelf; it does not identify MAME zips the way it identifies iNES.

## Vectrex (`vectrex`)

1982 vector cartridge. vecx. Alias: `gce`. Overlays are optional.

## ColecoVision (`colecovision`)

1982 cartridge. Gearcoleco (blueMSX as backup). Requires `coleco.rom`. Aliases: `coleco`, `cv`.

## Intellivision (`intellivision`)

1979 cartridge. FreeIntv. Requires `exec.bin` and `grom.bin` from an Intellivision you own. Alias: `intv`.

## PC Engine / TurboGrafx-16 (`pcengine`)

1987 HuCard. Aliases: `tg16`, `turbografx16`. Enabled.

## PC Engine CD (`pcenginecd`)

1988 disc. Separate id so System Card BIOS (`syscard3.pce`) is required only here. Aliases: `tgcd`, `turbografxcd`. HuCards stay in `pcengine`.

## Neo Geo (`neogeo`)

1990 AES / MVS cartridge. Enabled. BIOS is a romset of its own; see the manifest.

## 3DO (`3do`)

1993 disc. Opera. Requires `panafz10.bin`. Alias: `panasonic3do`.

## Neo Geo Pocket (`ngp`)

1998 cartridge (mono and color). Beetle NeoPop. Aliases: `ngpc`, `neogeopocket`.

## WonderSwan (`wonderswan`)

1999 cartridge (mono and color). Beetle WonderSwan. Aliases: `wsc`, `wonderswancolor`.

## Original Xbox (`xbox`)

2001 disc. Catalog / price-guide only. Alias: `ogxbox`.
